Trained with train_lnn_mnist.py (row-sequential MNIST + on-device affine & stroke-width augmentation for hand-drawn robustness). Because it reads sequentially, the prediction is often uncertain after the first few rows and sharpens as the digit's distinguishing strokes come into view — that temporal firming-up is the liquid network thinking.
