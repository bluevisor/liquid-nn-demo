A Liquid Time-Constant (LTC) network running its ODE solver in your browser, one frame at a time. Every neuron re-tunes its own time constant in response to the input — watch it happen live. Click any neuron to dissect its update.
continuous dynamics (Hasani et al. 2021)
dx/dt = −[ 1/τ + f(u,x) ]·x + f(u,x)·A
semi-implicit solver step (per sub-step h)
x ← x + h·f·A1 + h·(1/τ + f)
f(u,x)=σ(W[u,x]+b) is a learned gate. Because it sits inside the decay term, the effective time constant τ_eff = 1/(1/τ + f) shrinks when input is strong → the neuron speeds up. That input-dependence is the whole idea.

What you're looking at: a real LTC forward pass — no pre-baked animation. Each frame integrates the ODE by solver steps semi-implicit Euler sub-steps over interval dt. Weights are random (seeded), so this net isn't trained on a task — the point is the mechanism: crank amp up or hit Inject pulse and watch the τ_eff bars collapse (neurons speeding up); raise base τ × to make the whole net sluggish; drop solver steps to 1 with a big dt to see the integrator get coarse.
